AskMint

An embeddable ask box for static sites — no backend, no popup, no redirect service. Visitors type a question, your mail app opens with it pre-filled.

Put it on your site

  1. Copy widget.js into your site's files.
  2. Edit config.js — recipient email, greeting, categories, mode.
  3. Add <div id="askmint"></div> where you want it. Done.

Prefer JSON config? <div id="askmint" data-config="config.json"></div> works too. Delivery modes: email (default), link (any form endpoint with {text} and {category} placeholders), or copy (clipboard).
