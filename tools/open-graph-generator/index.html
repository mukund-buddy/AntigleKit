Questions

        
Open Graph FAQ

      

      

        

          What is Open Graph?
          
Open Graph is a protocol that lets any page describe itself via og: meta tags. WhatsApp, Facebook, X, and most other platforms read it and build the link preview from the title, description, and image you provide.

        

        

          Why does the preview look different from the debugger?
          
Platforms cache aggressively. After changing tags, refresh the share link through the platform's debugger (for example Facebook's Sharing Debugger) to request a fresh scrape.

        

        

          What size should the share image be?
          
1200×630px at a 1.91:1 ratio is the de-facto standard. Almost every platform supports it, and badly cropped previews are usually an off-ratio image.
