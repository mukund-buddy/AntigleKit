Questions

Downloader FAQ

Which sites can I download from?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X / Twitter, Reddit, Facebook, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Pinterest, Twitch clips, Bilibili, Dailymotion, Snapchat, Bluesky, Tumblr and more. Paste the link on the downloader page and the service is detected automatically.

How does this work without a backend?

AntigleKit is a static launcher — it doesn't download anything itself. It opens your link on cobalt.tools, the free open-source downloader, which does the work. AntigleKit never sees, stores, or proxies your link or your media.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. cobalt.tools runs in your browser with no account, no sign-up, and no extension. Paste the link and download.

Can I choose the quality or get audio only?

Yes — on cobalt.tools you pick the quality you want (up to 4K where available), or switch to audio-only to save just the sound as MP3, OGG, Opus, or WAV.

Is downloading videos allowed?

Download only content you have permission to save — your own uploads, freely licensed media, or wherever the platform and your local laws allow it. Always respect the creator's rights and the platform's terms.
