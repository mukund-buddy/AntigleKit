Questions

        
Case converter FAQ

      

      

        

          What is the difference between camelCase and PascalCase?
          
Both join words with a capital letter after the first: camelCase keeps the first letter lower (myVariable), PascalCase capitalises every word including the first (MyClass). Most languages reserve camelCase for variables and PascalCase for classes.

        

        

          Why does tOGGLE cASE look random?
          
It is the opposite of random: every second letter is flipped between lower and upper case. It is a playful chat style, not an error — paste it again to reverse the effect.

        

        

          Are leading spaces and punctuation preserved?
          
Yes. The input is never truncated and non-letter characters stay exactly where they were. Punctuation is not stripped.
