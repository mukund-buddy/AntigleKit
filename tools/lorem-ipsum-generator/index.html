Questions

        
Lorem Ipsum FAQ

      

      

        

          Is the text real Latin?
          
It descends from a scrambled section of Cicero dating to 45 BC, but the words are deliberately shuffled so the result reads like Latin without saying anything meaningful. Classic filler forever since the 1500s.

        

        

          Why does the first sentence have a comma?
          
The traditional opener “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit…” preserves the comma so the phrase looks like the canonical copy. Turn the classic opener off for simple sentences everywhere.

        

        

          Is there anywhere I should not use it?
          
Never in production-facing interface text such as labels, buttons, or error messages — that copy must be written and translated. Lorem Ipsum belongs in wireframes, prototypes and previews only.
