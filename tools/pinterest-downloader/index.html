Questions

Pinterest pin saver FAQ

How do I save a Pinterest image?

Paste the pin link, tap Preview pin, then save with your browser's own menu — right-click → Save image as… on a computer, press and hold → Save image on Android and iPhone. The file comes straight from Pinterest, untouched.

Does it work with video pins?

Yes — video pins play inside the preview through Pinterest's official player, with the original caption and Save button visible. Saving the video file itself is done with the save option inside the Pinterest app.

Why is there no direct Download button?

Pinterest serves its images and videos without the headers browsers need for a direct file download, and AntigleKit runs 100% in your browser with no server in between. Your browser's built-in save menu gives you the same original file.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. On Android and iPhone, press and hold the pin and choose Save Image. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the pin loads from Pinterest into your browser only.

Is the Pinterest pin saver free?

Yes. Every tool on AntigleKit is free and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or processed on a server.
